About Me

I'm an Applied Scientist at Amazon AGI working on evaluation, recursive self-improvement (RSI) and RL post-training of LLM agents. I build evaluations that expose where agents fail, generate the data that fixes those failures, and increasingly let models run that loop themselves.

Before Amazon, I did my graduate studies at ETH Zurich and UC Berkeley, where I was a researcher in the Sky Computing Lab with Ion Stoica and Joey Gonzalez. My CV is here (PDF).
